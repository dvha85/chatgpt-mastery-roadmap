# Boot Sample Brief — Dữ liệu mẫu

> Dữ liệu giả lập, dùng cho BOOT.2 và BOOT.3. Không đại diện cho khách hàng thật.

## Freelancer website brief

- Audience: independent freelancers in Vietnam.
- Problem: they want a simple website to explain their service and receive inquiries.
- Required pages: Home, Services, About, Contact.
- Language: Vietnamese first; English summary optional.
- Constraint: use plain language and keep the first version small.
- Unknown: no budget, platform, domain or deadline was provided.

## Safe claims

1. The audience is independent freelancers in Vietnam.
2. The brief lists four required pages.
3. The brief does not provide a budget.

## Unsupported examples

- A specific platform is best.
- The budget is 10 million VND.
- The project must launch this week.

Use the brief as a closed source. Mark unsupported examples as UNCERTAIN.
